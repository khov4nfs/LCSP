# LCSP Business Rules Specification

## Purpose

Tài liệu này chuẩn hóa business rules cho LCSP ở mức analysis/design trước implementation. Business rules bao gồm rule truyền thống của web/compliance platform và guardrail đặc thù LCSP. Đây không phải backlog.

## Scope

Áp dụng cho Authentication, Organization, RBAC authorization, Assessment lifecycle, Wizard, Evidence, Reconciliation, Scoring, Classification, Legal citation, Report, Audit, Security/Privacy và Notification/Task.

## Phase 5.2L Rule Corrections

- RBAC replaces RBAC as authorization source of truth. Existing role/permission rules must be read as subject attributes, grouping labels, policy templates or historical wording only.
- Human Attestation rules `BR-052..BR-056` are `SUPERSEDED_FOR_ACTIVE_MVP`.
- `BR-033` Local/CI report upload is superseded by `FR-050` Automatic Trusted Scan Initiation.
- `BR-034` manual evidence JSON upload is `REMOVED_FROM_PRODUCT`.
- Direct regulator submission, compliance certification and formal legal opinion are `REMOVED_FROM_PRODUCT`.
- New or remediated rules must evaluate subject, organization, resource, action, request/runtime context, policy and policy version; every material decision must be auditable with policy ID/version and correlation ID.

## Rule ID Convention

Business Rule ID dùng convention tuần tự toàn cục:

```text
BR-001
BR-002
BR-003
```

Không dùng prefix category; toàn bộ rule dùng ID tuần tự `BR-XXX`.

## Rule-to-Use-Case Traceability

Primary rule-to-use-case mapping is embedded in the detailed rule table. Full cross-matrix is maintained in `docs/specs/requirements-traceability-matrix.md`.

## Rule-to-Requirement Traceability

Every rule that constrains a functional behavior references a valid `FR-XXX`. Cross-cutting policies may reference `None` only when no single FR owns the rule.

## Rule-to-State Traceability

State-sensitive rules include `Related State`. State machine source remains `docs/specs/domain-state-machines.md`.

## Rule-to-Audit Traceability

Rules that affect access, RBAC decisions, trusted triggers, evidence, classification, documents or security controls define an audit event. Audit event names are normalized in specs and QA docs.

- (direction) Use the approved Incremental/Hybrid delivery path: preserve existing top-up wallet and telemetry, add shadow microcredit/pricing/admission/settlement, and keep authoritative debit disabled until G-01..G-07 are GREEN.
- (constraint) RBAC-only, owner/resource-scoped authorization; no PBAC or organization boundary; one WORKER_API_KEY; paid-tool billing deferred.
- (constraint) Current worker SettledUsagePayload and NestJS BillingUsageKernel are telemetry-only and must not become financial authority without immutable server admission.
- (decision) NestJS Billing is the sole financial authority; deepagents may request/consume admission and submit provider evidence but cannot choose wallet, tariff, charge amount or ledger effect.
- (decision) Each physical provider invocation has an immutable server-issued invocation identity and admission; retry/fallback creates a new invocation under the same logical operation.
- (decision) Published microcredit tariffs are immutable and pinned at admission; runtime settlement never fetches FX, applies markup or calculates customer tax.
- (decision) Authoritative financial effects are append-only, atomic and idempotent; customer balance is a signed projection with spendable=max(balance,0) and outstanding=max(-balance,0).
- (decision) OUTCOME_UNKNOWN retains admission exposure and enters bounded reconciliation; missing usage never means zero and compensation appends a bounded entry linked to the original debit.
- (constraint) Production debit rollout order is contract RED, shadow accounting/pricing, mandatory admission with debit off, shadow settlement/reconciliation, UX/operations rehearsal, then gated enablement.
- (decision) Every provider call declares CUSTOMER_WALLET or explicitly authorized PLATFORM_SPONSORED funding; ownerless calls without sponsorship fail before dispatch and platform sponsorship never debits a customer wallet.
- (decision) Admission uses stable request identity/fingerprint plus a one-time high-entropy capability bound to job/run/lease/invocation; NestJS/PostgreSQL commits IN_FLIGHT before provider bytes and crash at that boundary becomes DISPATCH_UNKNOWN without automatic replay.
- (decision) Customer tariff entities are distinct from legacy ModelPricingSnapshot reference-cost data; normalized provider/resolved-model scope and database time are shared by atomic publication and admission selection.
- (decision) Provider usage is normalized by a versioned mapping into non-overlapping billable buckets; inclusive/aggregate totals are evidence-only and ambiguity fails to integrity review without debit.
- (decision) The append-only microcredit ledger is accounting authority; signed wallet balance/version is a transactional projection and mismatch blocks financial writes.
- (decision) Rollout is one monotonic server-owned mode pinned on admission; shadow admission cannot become debit-authorized merely because a later process observes DEBIT_ENABLED.
- (decision) Financial contracts are expand-only and versioned in packages/contracts; unsupported versions fail before mutation and legacy telemetry adapters cannot synthesize financial authority.
- (decision) Admission proof is a high-entropy single-admission capability: server stores digest; worker may persist only an encrypted recovery envelope until terminal acknowledgement; replay safety comes from state and canonical fingerprints.
- (decision) PLATFORM_SPONSORED pins a PlatformSponsorshipPolicySnapshot and writes provider cost/exposure/audit/outbox only, never customer tariff, ledger, outstanding or payment-required state.
- (decision) Emergency rollout rollback revokes unconsumed customer admissions; IN_FLIGHT work is not replayed and either settles under pinned eligible authority or enters integrity review under an explicit financial freeze.
- (decision) Financial lock order is wallet/sponsorship aggregate, admission, original debit/reconciliation case, then projection/outbox.
- (event) spine finalized

# Controls and Claims Verification

## Use when
A selected criterion depends on a claimed safeguard or operational control.

## Investigation
- Convert relevant product claims into falsifiable reachable execution questions.
- Verify inputs, validations, branches, side effects and persistence.
- Contrast README, UI, mocks and imports with actual code paths.
- Challenge source-derived results through counterexamples and error cases.
- Identify what must be resolved from external operator evidence.

## Non-authority
Do not invent mandatory safeguards or legal outcomes from technical findings alone.

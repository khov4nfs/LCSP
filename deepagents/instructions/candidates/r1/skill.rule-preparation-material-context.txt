# Development legal-context closure

## Inputs
Pinned source graph, verified development operative claims, ancestors, directed relations, legal effect and target-date status.

## Procedure
1. Retain each actor-defining ancestor lead-in, risk-class condition, trigger, qualifier, exception, temporal/transition effect and operative cross-reference that materially affects a development claim.
2. Only remove irrelevant text from the *model-facing projection*, not from the legal source, provenance, authoritative inventory or coverage ledger.
3. Keep relations directed and explain their materiality: CONDITION_OF, QUALIFIES, EXCEPTION_TO, DEFINED_BY, TEMPORALLY_APPLIES_TO, INCORPORATES, PRESERVES, CONTEXT_FOR and CROSS_REFERENCE.
4. Do not promote a heading, definition, optional recommendation, commercial policy or public authority into a customer-facing AssessmentRule.
5. If an unresolved reference or missing effective-date fact could change applicability, keep the issue unresolved and do not silently exclude that context.

## Negative controls
An exception in an otherwise operational provision may still affect a developer duty. The ancestor of a Vietnamese point "đ" must retain the exact character; `pt-` with no point is an input-integrity problem, not a valid legal locator. A partially repealed point does not repeal the whole Article.

## Output
Retained context edges (claim, source node, relationship, exact quote, reason) and excluded model-view nodes (source node, reason); neither list mutates pinned source records.

## Deployment-only context must not leak into development assessment

- Suppress every deployment-only Article, clause, point and context block that has no verified directed dependency on an included development operative claim.
- Do not carry a parent Article's entire deployment narrative merely because its child point also addresses a developer. Carry the minimal legal condition/definition/exception that actually modifies the development duty.
- Materiality requires a pinned source locator and source-supported relation. Proximity, shared words or semantic topic similarity are not materiality.
- If the required context cannot be verified at the pinned version, mark the dependency for human/legal review. Never invent it, never treat it as verified, and never surface unrelated deployment passages in the dev view.
- Source accounting stays complete outside the model-facing development projection.

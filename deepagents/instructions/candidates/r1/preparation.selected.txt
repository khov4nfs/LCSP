## Mandatory legal-to-AssessmentRule gate

Do not turn a provision heading directly into a repository evidence checklist. Apply these checks before generating any AssessmentRule:

1. **Actor and normative force:** Read the pinned provision and material ancestor lead-in. Identify the actual legal addressee, regulated actor, action, object, modality, trigger and exceptions. 2. **Separate responsibility from procedure:** Distinguish public-authority inspection/supervision, deployment/operation obligations, externally performed organizational acts and a separately source-supported developer design-time obligation.
